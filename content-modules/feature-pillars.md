# MealPlanner — Feature pillars

## Core engine
- Builds a 7-day meal plan from goal and dietary restrictions
- Keeps meals simple enough to actually cook
- Copy-or-print output for kitchen or grocery runs

## Workflow & output
- People who want to eat better without spreadsheet planning
- Respects restrictions rather than a generic menu
- Week of real meals, not inspiration you never cook

## Trust & control
- Decision-support for meal planning — not medical or dietetic advice
- No fabricated health-outcome claims
- Demo mode without a live API key
